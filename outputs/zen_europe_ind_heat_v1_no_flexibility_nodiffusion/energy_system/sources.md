carbon_emissions_budget
-----------------------

Step 1
Extended Mannhardt (2026) Appendix A.2 carbon budget (22.586810 Gt) to credit glass/ceramic/paper/food sectors using variant 'B' (E_old=2563680.158 kt, 28 countries; E_new=158890.730 kt, 28 countries + UK via BEIS2023/ONS2026). See ASSUMPTIONS.md 'Carbon emissions budget' for the three compared variants and the double-counting caveat for glass/ceramics.

**Citations**

- **mannhardt_2026**: Mannhardt, J. (2026). Emissions and carbon budget (Appendix A.2). PhD dissertation, ETH Zurich.
- **eea_unfccc_2022**: European Environment Agency (2026). UNFCCC Common Reporting Format GHG inventory data. EEA GHG data viewer (UNFCCC_v30 export, 2022 data).
- **beis_2023**: Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy (2023). Final UK greenhouse gas emissions national statistics: 1990 to 2021. gov.uk statistical release (accessed 2023-06-19).
- **ons_2026**: Office for National Statistics (2026). Atmospheric emissions: greenhouse gases by industry and gas. ONS dataset (released 2026-06-05).
